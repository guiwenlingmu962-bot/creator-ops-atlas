PRIVACY

Collect less. Keep less. Expose less.

Last reviewed: 2026-06-28.

Starter-site behavior

This static starter site has no analytics, cookies, accounts, database, upload form, advertising scripts, or server-side contact form. Consultation links open the public Freelancer profile or a public GitHub issue form; those services have their own privacy terms.

Do not send sensitive data

Do not email passwords, API keys, government IDs, tax records, payment details, customer addresses, real customer exports, explicit media, or performer records. Use synthetic examples for the first review.

Before publishing

The operator must replace the placeholder contact address, add truthful business details required in the target jurisdiction, document any hosting logs or analytics, set retention periods, and provide a contact method for privacy and safety reports.

External links

External sites have their own privacy and tracking practices. Opening an official-source link leaves CreatorOps Atlas.
